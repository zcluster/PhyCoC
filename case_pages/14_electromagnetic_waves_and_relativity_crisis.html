Central claim#

Hertz confirmed Maxwellian waves, while precision experiments and electrodynamics exposed a conflict: Maxwell's equations single out speed \(c\), whereas Galilean transformations change velocities by addition. Ether-drift searches did not reveal the expected preferred-frame motion. Lorentzian mathematical repairs prepared, but did not by themselves complete, Einstein's kinematic reframing.

Historical problem#

Before the focal discovery (1887–1904 (Hertz through Lorentz's mature electron theory)), the case confronted a linked set of pressures: Electromagnetic waves implied; Sparks generate and detect radio-frequency waves. The pathways R-GALILEAN-ELECTRODYNAMICS, R-RIGID-STATIONARY-ETHER, R-FULLY-DRAGGED-ETHER, R-LORENTZ-ETHER-THEORY were historically reasonable attempts to organize parts of the problem, but they did not jointly satisfy the empirical, mathematical, and explanatory constraints represented by the time slices and knowledge assets. The discovery task in Wave propagation, ether tests, and electrodynamics of moving bodies was to construct a more generative account without importing later validation evidence into the original inference.

Alternative, incomplete, or superseded pathways#

R-GALILEAN-ELECTRODYNAMICS#

  • What it is: The attempt to combine Maxwellian electromagnetism with Galilean space and absolute time, transforming velocities by simple subtraction between inertial frames.
  • Proposed/active period: pre-1887 classical kinematic extrapolation.
  • Assumption: \(\mathbf{u}'=\mathbf{u}-\mathbf{v}\) for all signals.
  • Limitation: A light wave with speed \(c\) would have frame-dependent speed, conflicting with Maxwellian form and experiment.
  • Outcome: Replaced by Lorentz transformations.

R-RIGID-STATIONARY-ETHER#

  • What it is: A theory in which electromagnetic waves propagate through a rigid or effectively stationary luminiferous ether that defines an absolute rest frame relative to which Earth moves.
  • Proposed/active period: nineteenth century.
  • Why reasonable: Waves were known to require media; an ether supplied a rest frame.
  • Anomaly: No robust first-order or expected second-order ether wind.
  • Repair: Drag hypotheses, contraction, local time.
  • Outcome: Preferred mechanical ether became unnecessary.
  • Retained element: Fields remain real, but not as motion of a classical substance.

R-FULLY-DRAGGED-ETHER#

  • What it is: An ether model in which matter carries the nearby optical medium with it, suppressing a local ether wind.
  • Proposed/active period: 1845 (Stokes-type dragging).
  • Outcome: Conflicted with stellar aberration and moving-medium results; rejected.

R-LORENTZ-ETHER-THEORY#

  • What it is: A stationary-ether theory in which motion physically contracts bodies and alters clock readings so experiments obey Lorentz transformations while an unobservable preferred frame remains.
  • Proposed/active period: 1892–1904.
  • Outcome: Empirically sophisticated but explanatorily superseded by special relativity; Lorentz mathematics retained.

Transformative move#

For an interferometer with arm length \(L\) moving at hypothesized ether speed \(v\), naive ether kinematics predicts a round-trip time difference of order:

\[\Delta t\approx \frac{Lv^2}{c^3}\]

between parallel and transverse arms, hence a fringe shift:

\[\Delta N\sim\frac{c\Delta t}{\lambda} \sim\frac{Lv^2}{\lambda c^2}.\]

The expected Earth-orbital-scale signal was not observed. A null result alone does not logically prove special relativity, but it constrained simple ether models.

Lorentz transformations preserve:

\[x'=\gamma(x-vt), \qquad t'=\gamma\left(t-\frac{vx}{c^2}\right), \qquad \gamma=\frac{1}{\sqrt{1-v^2/c^2}},\]

and the interval \(c^2t^2-x^2\). The crisis was resolved by treating these as spacetime relations, not merely dynamical distortions through ether.

Validation and explanatory gains#

  • Hertz demonstrated reflection, refraction, interference, and polarization of radio waves.
  • Ether-drift tests increasingly constrained anisotropy.
  • Stellar aberration and moving-source electrodynamics required a consistent transformation theory.
  • Special relativity unified the successful electromagnetic results with a new mechanics.

Limitations and retained status#

Michelson–Morley was not a single decisive overthrow, and Lorentz and Poincaré made essential contributions. Modern Lorentz-invariance tests far exceed 1887 precision. Electromagnetic waves remain classical solutions, while photons provide the quantum description of radiation.

Extended historical investigation#

Hertz's experiment as a prediction test#

Hertz produced oscillating sparks in a transmitter and detected induced sparks in a separate loop. He investigated reflection, refraction, polarization, interference, and standing-wave behavior. Measuring wavelength \(\lambda\) and oscillator frequency \(f\) permits:

\[v=f\lambda.\]

The agreement with optical light speed supported Maxwell's prediction that the new radiation and light belonged to the same electromagnetic family. This was not merely an applied invention of radio; it was a test of a field theory in a previously unobserved frequency regime.

Why Maxwell and Galilean kinematics conflict#

The one-dimensional wave equation:

\[\frac{\partial^2\psi}{\partial x^2} -\frac{1}{c^2} \frac{\partial^2\psi}{\partial t^2}=0\]

does not retain the same form under:

\[x'=x-vt, \qquad t'=t.\]

If speeds simply add, one expects \(c'=c-v\). Maxwell's theory instead has a fixed characteristic speed. Nineteenth-century physics could respond by:

  1. privileging an ether rest frame;
  2. modifying electrodynamics for moving bodies;
  3. modifying space and time transformations.

The eventual choice was not logically forced by one null result; it emerged through a network of theory and experiment.

Michelson–Morley reconstruction#

For arm length \(L\) parallel to a hypothesized ether wind \(v\), classical travel time is:

\[t_\parallel =\frac{L}{c-v}+\frac{L}{c+v} =\frac{2Lc}{c^2-v^2}.\]

For the transverse arm:

\[t_\perp =\frac{2L}{\sqrt{c^2-v^2}}.\]

Expanding for \(v\ll c\):

\[t_\parallel \approx\frac{2L}{c} \left(1+\frac{v^2}{c^2}\right),\]
\[t_\perp \approx\frac{2L}{c} \left(1+\frac12\frac{v^2}{c^2}\right).\]

Thus:

\[\Delta t \approx\frac{Lv^2}{c^3}.\]

Rotation should change the phase difference:

\[\Delta N\sim\frac{c\Delta t}{\lambda}.\]

The expected simple ether-wind signal was not found at the anticipated scale. The experiment had nonzero residuals and complex systematics; “perfect null” is an oversimplification.

Lorentz–FitzGerald repair and the theory-choice problem#

If the arm parallel to motion contracts:

\[L_\parallel=L_0\sqrt{1-\frac{v^2}{c^2}},\]

the predicted difference can disappear. Lorentz developed local time and transformations within an electron/ether theory; Poincaré sharpened the relativity principle and transformation group. Einstein's 1905 formulation removed the need to interpret contraction and local time as dynamical deformations caused by motion through ether.

These formulations can share many observable predictions. Theory choice involved explanatory structure, symmetry, and what entities were treated as fundamental. A knowledge graph should preserve empirical equivalence where it existed rather than saying Michelson–Morley directly derived Einstein's postulates.

Relativity of electric and magnetic fields#

Under a Lorentz boost, fields mix. In a simple perpendicular configuration:

\[\mathbf E'_\perp =\gamma(\mathbf E_\perp+\mathbf v\times\mathbf B),\]
\[\mathbf B'_\perp =\gamma\left( \mathbf B_\perp -\frac{1}{c^2}\mathbf v\times\mathbf E \right).\]

What is mostly electric in one frame may include magnetism in another. This supplies a deeper unification: magnetism is not a separate substance but part of the relativistic electromagnetic field tensor.

Retention map#

Galilean transformation remains the leading approximation:

\[\gamma =1+\frac12\frac{v^2}{c^2} +\mathcal O(v^4/c^4).\]

Ether language survives in some effective media—condensed-matter quasiparticles can propagate relative to a material rest frame—but this is not the universal luminiferous ether of nineteenth-century electrodynamics.

Additional quantitative and epistemic notes#

Additional quantitative and epistemic notes#

Hertz's sparks in a receiving loop provided laboratory evidence that oscillating electrical systems radiate and that the radiation reflects, refracts, interferes, and propagates at a speed consistent with light. This validated Maxwellian waves without yet settling how motion through a supposed ether should transform fields.

The Michelson–Morley interferometer targeted an orientation-dependent travel-time difference. For arm length \(L\) and hypothesized ether speed \(v\), the expected leading fringe shift scaled as

\[\Delta N\sim\frac{2L}{\lambda}\frac{v^2}{c^2}.\]

The null result constrained simple stationary-ether models, but did not alone derive special relativity. Lorentz–FitzGerald contraction and Lorentz's electron theory were serious repairs that reproduced key null effects. Einstein's 1905 contribution reframed the transformation as spacetime kinematics based on relativity and invariant light speed. Thus HERTZ-CONFIRMATION, ETHER-ANOMALIES, LORENTZ-REPAIR, and EINSTEIN-REFRAMING should remain separate nodes.
